    How it works

    Notes scroll toward the glowing line. The number is the fret, the color is the left-hand finger, and the small letter is the right-hand finger (p thumb, i index, m middle, a ring). Tap the legend in the corner to see the colors.

    Modes

    • Play: scrolls at the tempo you pick. With the mic on, each note turns green when it hears you on time, or red if missed.
    • Wait: stops at each note until you play it, like the Gibson app. No mic? Tap ✓ (or press Enter) to step through.
    • Edit: drag the highway to move around, tap a note to change its string, fret, fingers, length or timing. Turn on ✚ Add and tap an empty spot to add a note. Changes save on this device. Undo is there if you slip.

    Loading a piece

    • MusicXML (best): free scores on MuseScore can be downloaded or exported as MusicXML (.mxl or .musicxml). If the score has a tab staff, the strings and frets come through exactly. If not, the app picks sensible strings, frets and fingers, and you fix what you'd play differently.
    • PDF: a PDF can't be read directly. Convert it to MusicXML first with MuseScore's free PDF import or the free Audiveris app, then import that. Or paste the tab as text.
    • Text tab: paste ASCII tab (the e|---0--- kind). Timing is guessed from the spacing, so check it in Edit mode.

    Memorizing

    Loop a few bars, then use Settings → Memorize to hide the fret numbers, fade the notes, or go blind. In Wait mode the app still checks every note.

    Mic tips

    • Single notes are detected well. Chords are harder: by default a chord counts if any of its notes is heard. Turn on “Chord helper” and “Every note must be heard” for a stricter check.
    • Turn Guide off while the mic is on (or use headphones), or the app hears itself.
    • Keep the phone a foot or two from the guitar in a quiet room.

    Keep your songs safe

    Everything is stored only in this browser on this device. On iPhone, add the page to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) and use Songs → “Back up all” once in a while, because Safari can clear website storage you haven't opened in a few weeks.
